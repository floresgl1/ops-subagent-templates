Your scheduled job ran last night. Did it actually work?

Scheduled systems fail silently. You want a daily sanity check without building a full monitoring stack.

This template gives you a two-layer diagnostic pattern: a health-snapshot script gathers the facts, then a Claude sub-agent reads the results, reasons about what happened, and delivers a structured verdict—HEALTHY, DEGRADED, or FAILED.

Two-layer diagnostic pattern

health-snapshot.py gathers facts from your system: output file existence, task exit codes, cross-check invariants. It runs standalone with no AI dependencies.

The daily-triage agent reads the health output, reasons about what happened, diagnoses issues, and posts a verdict to your webhook.

Read-only by design. The sub-agent can't modify files, hit APIs, or change your system. It only reads and reports. Belt and suspenders: tool scoping plus a hard rule in the agent prompt.
